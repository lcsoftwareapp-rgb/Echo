Echo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Echo is a personal reminder app for Android. It listens for commitments you make out loud, turns them into reminders, and nudges you until you act on them.

Echo is built to keep your data on your phone. Audio, transcripts, calendar events, and reminders are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The only personal information that leaves your phone is the email address you use to create an account.

What Echo collects, and where it goes

DataWhyWhere it goes
Microphone audio To detect commitments you speak aloud. Stays on your device. Speech recognition and analysis run entirely on your phone using on-device models. Recordings are deleted once processed. Audio is never uploaded, stored remotely, or transmitted anywhere.
Transcripts and reminders The commitments Echo extracts, and the reminders you approve. Stays on your device, in local app storage. This version of Echo has no cloud sync — your reminders are not sent to any server.
Calendar events (optional) To nudge you before upcoming events, if you grant calendar access. Stays on your device. Events are read locally and never transmitted.
Email address To create and sign in to your account. Stored by our authentication provider, Supabase. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google. This is the only personal data that leaves your device.
Model downloads Echo downloads its speech and language models after install. A standard file download from our hosting provider. Like any web request, the server records the IP address and time of the request. These downloads are not tied to your account.

What Echo does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services. Echo contains no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information with third parties.
  • No uploading of your audio, transcripts, reminders, or calendar data.
  • No reading of your notifications, email, or messages in this version of the app.

Permissions Echo requests

  • Microphone — to listen for spoken commitments. Echo shows an on-screen indicator whenever it is listening, and you can turn listening off at any time.
  • Notifications — to deliver your reminders.
  • Calendar (optional) — to read upcoming events for proactive nudges.
  • Alarms and full-screen notifications — so high-priority reminders can reach you on the lock screen at the exact time you set.
  • Foreground service and wake lock — to keep listening and reminder delivery reliable while the app runs in the background.

How long data is kept

Audio recordings are deleted as soon as they have been processed on your device. Transcripts and reminders remain on your phone until you delete them, clear your capture history from the Privacy section of your profile, or uninstall the app. Your email address is kept until you delete your account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app: Profile → your account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account record and wipes all Echo data stored on your device.

Full instructions, including how to request deletion if you can no longer sign in, are on the account deletion page.

Children

Echo is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Service providers

Echo uses Supabase for account authentication and for hosting the model files the app downloads. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google processes your sign-in. We do not use any other third-party processors.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. Because your reminders and transcripts live only on your phone, you already hold that data directly. To exercise any right relating to your account, use in-app deletion or contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be surfaced in the app.
