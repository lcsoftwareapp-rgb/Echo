Delete Your Echo Account

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your Echo account and the data associated with it. It applies to the Echo Android app, published by Leon Chai.

Delete from inside the app

The fastest way is directly in Echo:

  1. Open Echo and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap your account at the top of the screen.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery period and no way to restore a deleted account.

You can also reach this from Settings. Open Settings → Account → Delete account.

Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled Echo, lost access to your account, or cannot sign in for any reason, email us and we will delete your account for you:

lc.softwareapp@gmail.com

Send the request from the email address you used to sign up, so we can verify the account belongs to you. We will confirm deletion within 30 days, and in most cases much sooner.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account record and email address Permanently deleted from our authentication provider.
Reminders, transcripts, and capture history Erased from your device. This data is only ever stored on your phone, so deleting your account removes all of it.
Audio recordings None are retained. Echo deletes recordings on your device as soon as they are processed, so there is nothing left to delete.
Calendar data None is retained. Echo reads your calendar on the device and never stores or transmits a copy.

Data kept after deletion

We do not retain any personal data after your account is deleted. Our hosting provider may keep routine server logs, which can include IP addresses, for a short period as part of normal operation and abuse prevention. These logs are not linked to your account once it is deleted.

Deleting the app without deleting your account

Uninstalling Echo removes all app data stored on your phone, but it does not delete your account. To remove your account as well, use one of the two methods above.
